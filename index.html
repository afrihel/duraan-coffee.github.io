Our coffee

Green coffee. Two distinctive processes.

We export unroasted green coffee for importers, traders, and roasters. Natural and washed lots are offered subject to availability and agreement.

N
Process option 01

Natural Process

Coffee cherries are dried with the fruit around the seed. The resulting green coffee is prepared for export without roasting. Lot details are confirmed per inquiry.

W
Process option 02

Washed Process

Coffee is pulped and washed during processing, then dried and prepared as green coffee for export without roasting. Details depend on the lot offered.

Important: We export green, unroasted coffee only. Natural and washed describe how the coffee is processed—not roasted coffee or roast levels.
